AICORD SOCIAL MEDIA PACK
========================
Every profile picture is the same full-bleed icon, sized per platform. The mark sits inside the circular
safe area, so circle crops (Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, WhatsApp, Telegram, Twitch,
Reddit, Facebook, Bluesky) never cut it off.

FOLDERS
_all-profile-pictures   every profile picture in one folder
_all-banners            every banner in one folder
<platform>/             everything for that platform (templates, stories, covers)
bios.txt                ready-to-paste bios, character counts checked

NOTES
- Sizes are what I know of each platform's recommendations and they change. Check the upload hint when you upload.
- Files are PNG because most platforms do not accept WebP uploads.
- On X, Facebook, LinkedIn and Twitch the profile picture overlaps the bottom-left of the banner, so the
  logo on every banner is centered.
